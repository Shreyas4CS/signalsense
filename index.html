How the project works

Four training steps, then go live

Collect examples, label them, train the AI, and watch your smart signal make decisions on its own.

STEP 1

Vehicles

Capture photos of your toys, then label each one with its vehicle type.

STEP 2

Road line

STEP 3

Sounds

Record short clips of horns, sirens and plain background noise.

STEP 4

Train the AI

Pick KNN or a Neural Network, train it, and check its accuracy.

Two modes

Teach it first, then let it run the road

Everything you train is saved in this browser, even after a refresh, until you press Forget data.

Training mode

Feed the machine data

You are the teacher. The more varied your examples, the smarter the signal gets.

  • Camera on/off and switch between cameras
  • Label every vehicle photo with a name
  • Point out the stop line on a road photo
  • Record and name traffic sounds with a live dB meter
Live mode

The signal thinks for itself

Google MediaPipe tracks every vehicle and draws a box around it, even ones you never trained.

  • Boxes and names on every vehicle it sees
  • Red jumpers across the line add red time
  • Honking above your dB limit adds red time
  • Countdown on the 4-digit display, gates open and close
Two ways to learn

Nearest neighbours, or a neural network

Both start the same way: MediaPipe turns each vehicle into 1024 numbers that describe its shape and colour. Then the brain decides.

Way 1

K-Nearest Neighbours

Remembers every photo. A new vehicle is compared with all of them, and the k most similar photos vote.

# new toy on the road
closest 3 photos → Car, Car, Bus
vote → Car (67%)
# great with very few photos
Way 2 · recommended

Neural Network

Learns which numbers really matter for each type, so it copes better with toys it has never seen.

🤔

Will it know a car it has never seen?

Boxing and tracking: yes, always. MediaPipe finds vehicles without your training, so the stop-line and red-jump rules work for every vehicle. Naming it: only if it looks like what you taught. If you only trained “Car” with one red car, a blue truck may show as “Car (not trained)”. To make it work on new toys:

  1. Make groups by type (Car, Bus, Ambulance), not by one toy.
  2. Put 3–4 different toys in each group, from all sides.
  3. Use the Neural Network brain, and test with a toy you kept aside.
Your rules

You decide how the signal behaves

Rules read like sentences. Change the numbers, switch them on and off, or write your own.

🚨
WHEN · THEN

A vehicle crosses the line on red → add 5 s to red, gate stays shut.

📢
WHEN · THEN

Honking louder than 80 dB on red → add 3 s to red.

🚑
WHEN · THEN

The AI sees an Ambulance → switch to green right away.

🚗
WHEN · THEN

3 or more vehicles waiting on red → switch to green.

Step 1 · Teach the AI your vehicles

Make one group per type of vehicle (Car, Bus, Ambulance…). Then capture photos and label them.

Step 2 · Mark the stop line

If any vehicle crosses this line on red, the AI notices and your rules kick in.

🛣️
1. Point the camera at your road. 2. Press Take road photo. 3. Tap two points where the line is. 4. Name it and press Save line.

Settings & Rules

How long each light stays on. The 4-digit display counts down these seconds.

“Longest allowed” stops rules from making a light last forever. A red light can never be stretched beyond this.

Gate 1 follows the signal: open on green/yellow, closed on red. Gate 2 does the opposite (or the same, if you choose). Set the servo angles and press Test to try them on the Arduino.

Rules are “When ___ happens, do ___”. Make your own or start with a ready-made one:

Microphone

Laptop mics are not real sound meters. Adjust so a quiet room shows about 35–45 dB.

Sounds quieter than this are ignored (the AI says “Quiet”).

Camera AI

Higher = the AI only names a vehicle when it is very sure; otherwise it shows “not trained”. Lower it if your new toys are not being named.

Everything you train is saved inside this browser and stays after refresh. It is only deleted when you press Forget data.

Changes are saved in this browser. Press the button to send them to the Arduino.

Forget all data?

This deletes every vehicle photo, sound clip, road line, rule and setting. You will need to train the AI again.
