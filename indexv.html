Mark the stop line on the road

If a vehicle crosses this line while the light is red, the AI notices and your rules kick in.

🛣️
1. Point the camera at your road. 2. Press Take road photo. 3. Tap two points on the photo where the line is. 4. Name it and press Save line.

Settings & Rules

How long each light stays on. The 4-digit display counts down these seconds.

“Longest allowed” stops rules from making a light last forever. A red light can never be stretched beyond this.

Gate 1 follows the signal: open on green/yellow, closed on red. The other gates can do the opposite. Set the servo angles for open and closed, and press Test to try them on the Arduino.

Rules are “When ___ happens, do ___”. Make your own or start with a ready-made one:

Microphone

Laptop mics are not real sound meters. Adjust so a quiet room shows about 35–45 dB.

Sounds quieter than this are ignored (the AI says “Quiet”).

Camera AI

Higher strictness = the AI only names a vehicle when it is very sure. Otherwise it shows “Unknown vehicle”.

Everything you train is saved inside this browser and stays after refresh. It is only deleted when you press Forget data.

Changes are sent to the Arduino when you press Save.

Forget all data?

This deletes every vehicle photo, sound clip, road line, rule and setting. You will need to train the AI again.
